Common Issues

Cannot connect to VPN

Ensure you are using the latest GlobalProtect client (v6.1.x). Check that your credentials are correct and your account is not locked.

Slow VPN performance

Try switching between Gateway and Gateway-DR portals. Contact NOC if latency exceeds 200ms.

Certificate errors

Import the SandStorm Telecom Root CA from the Downloads page. Ensure system clock is synchronized.

HIP check failures

Ensure your antivirus definitions are current and disk encryption is enabled. Contact helpdesk for exemptions.
